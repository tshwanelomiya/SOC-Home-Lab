# Simulated Phishing Compromise Investigation

## Scenario
A controlled scenario was used to investigate a compromise beginning with a phishing email and progressing to unauthorized access to sensitive information.

## Investigation Workflow
1. Identify phishing indicators.
2. Review email headers and authentication results.
3. Investigate suspicious links, attachments and file indicators.
4. Review endpoint and authentication telemetry.
5. Determine affected accounts and systems.
6. Contain compromised accounts and endpoints.
7. Assess potential impact.
8. Recommend recovery and security-hardening measures.
9. Document lessons learned and detection improvements.

## Controls Considered
- Multi-factor authentication
- Email authentication controls such as SPF and DMARC
- Endpoint detection and monitoring
- Account isolation and credential reset
- File/folder access controls
- Security awareness and phishing defenses
