Technical controls to implement
·	file or folder level encryption: Encode the file or folder to allow only authorized users to have access to it. this effective in preventing unauthorized access with little to no challenges of implementation for SafariPay
·	Multi Factor authentication: A type of authentication that requires 2 or more methods of identification to ensure you are who you say you are. Effective as the methods to gain access can be challenging for the attacker to get. No challenges to SafariPay.
·	Email authentication: this ensures that the received email is not a spoofed. this involves checking the email headers and the SPF or DMARC, If one of them says failed then there is a high chance that the email received is part of a phishing attempt
